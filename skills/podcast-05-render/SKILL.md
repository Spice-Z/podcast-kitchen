# Step 5: render and loudness
Before render, `gate.py` mutes each speaker track outside its own speech regions (VAD ∪ laughs ∪ backchannels, with transcript words counted only where they overlap own VAD islands — alignment smear beyond VAD is dropped; pad 150/250 ms anchored to VAD speech edges, merge < 300 ms, 15 ms fades, `gate.attenuation_db`), classifies transients as low voicing AND noise-like high band (`gate.hb_share_min` share above 2 kHz or `gate.centroid_min_hz`; breathy low-band word endings and falling voiced decays are kept; `gate.word_end_keep_s` after a VAD end at a word end is protected), scans 100 ms windows inside every VAD island (muting high-band unvoiced windows with no voiced window within `gate.surround_s`; mostly-unvoiced islands ≤ `gate.island_sniff_max_s` are muted whole and their words are not firm support), separately mutes cough/throat-clear bursts (band-independent `gate.burst_*`: island ≤ `burst_max_s`, no firm text and no aligned word token covering half of it, onset ≥ a per-track rise within `burst_onset_ms` starting near the track floor — with `burst_relative` (default) the rise is `burst_onset_snr_frac` × the track's own SNR (speech p50 − floor) and the start margin is clamp(own non-speech p90 − floor, `burst_floor_min_db`, `burst_floor_max_db`), computed automatically per track and logged in logs/gate.json `burst_thresholds` (fixed `burst_onset_db`/`burst_floor_db` are only the fallback), unstable pitch — median frame voicing < `burst_voicing_max` or f0 jump > `burst_jump_max` — whole-island voicing < `burst_island_voicing_max`, and ≥ `burst_gap_s` after the speaker's own voiced speech so decays are kept; logged as `transients_burst`), gates short transient VAD islands without firm text (a word mostly inside the island) unless laughter/backchannel, mutes unvoiced bursts inside regions but outside voiced VAD (pad zones, merged gaps; sub-window classify) and unvoiced runs ≥ `gate.edge_tail_min_s` glued to a voiced island's head/tail (keeping `gate.edge_keep_s` next to speech), and fills room tone on `gate.fill_track` where every track is gated (room tone window = quietest VAD-free and burst-free window; source in logs/gate.json `roomtone_source`); render reads `work/gate/` and takes room tone from the ungated levelled tracks. `render.py` applies only approved edits (snap ±`render.snap_window_ms`, `render.crossfade_ms` equal-power on all tracks, room tone, `render.duck_db`), writes `work/mix.wav` (dual-mono) and `work/timemap.json`. `jingle.py` (config `jingle`, runs after render, before ending_bgm) inserts the mid-show jingle exactly once per episode (`jingle.file`, outside the repo): it prefers the pause at a chapter boundary (chapters_raw.json mapped to the edited timeline; gap within `chapter_before_s`/`chapter_after_s` of the chapter, the chapter closest to the episode midpoint first), otherwise the longest clean pause (speaker change preferred). A pause = hole in all speakers' VAD ≥ `min_gap_s`, no transcript segment running through it (`segment_tolerance_s`), the previous line ends a sentence (。！ or です/ます/ですね/よね… — not a question), the next line is not a reply (そう/うん/はい…), ≥ `avoid_margin_s` from laughter/backchannel/overlap; never before `min_start_s` (120 s) or within `exclude_end_s` (180 s, at least the ending-BGM search window) of the end. The pause is replaced by `pre_silence_s` (0.4) digital silence → jingle (level-matched to the voices' active RMS + `gain_db`) → `post_silence_s` (0.2) silence; time is only added, no speech removed. The timemap spans/joins after it are shifted, so chapters, transcript_edited, ending BGM and QA follow automatically (QA doesn't count the jingle as a long pause and doesn't click-check its edges). Pre-jingle mix/timemap are kept (`work/mix_prejingle.wav`, `timemap_prejingle.json`) so re-runs never stack; `--dry-run` only reports the spot; disabled/missing file/no pause → warning, pass-through. Log `logs/jingle.json`; report the edited timestamp and why. Search is tiered: candidates inside `prefer_range_frac` (default 0.3–0.7 of the edited duration) first; if none qualify, the range widens by `widen_step_frac` (0.1) per side per step up to the hard limits; within a tier chapter boundary > natural pause (the log reason names the tier). `ending_bgm.py` (config `ending_bgm`, runs after jingle, before loudness) finds the LAST closing phrase (`ending_bgm.phrases`, default 「ということで」) starting within `search_window_s` of the end of the edited transcript, fades the BGM (`ending_bgm.file`, outside the repo; absolute or relative to the episode dir) in from `lead_in_s` before it over `fade_in_s` to a bed `bed_db` below the voices' active level (measured over `voice_ref_window_s` before the last speech end), and after the last speech end on the edited timeline (VAD islands ≥ `min_island_s`, all speakers) ramps over `ramp_s` to `tail_db`, plays BGM alone until `tail_s` after the last speech and fades out over `fade_out_s` (7 s) linearly in dB from `tail_db` to `fade_out_floor_db` (−60 dB re voice), then gain 0 plus `end_silence_s` (1.5 s) of digital silence (QA edge/tail silence checks stop at the fade start), extending the episode; the voice mix is kept as `work/mix_voice.wav` so re-runs never stack BGM; `enabled: false`, a missing file or no phrase → warning and the voice mix passes through unchanged; log `logs/ending_bgm.json`, timemap gets `ending_bgm` + new `edited_duration_s`. Report where the phrase was found. QA judges joins inside the BGM span against the local 99.9th-percentile step (`qa.click_ratio_bgm`) instead of the median, so music attacks aren't reported as clicks. `loudness.py`: ffmpeg loudnorm two-pass linear to `loudness.target_lufs` / `loudness.max_true_peak_dbtp`, fails if not linear; writes only the 192k stereo MP3 (temp WAVs deleted after encoding; no WAV master). Structural edits `opening_chatter` (cut small talk before the first theme line of the Zencastr transcript) and `edge_trim` (head/tail silence to `structure.edge_silence_s`) are added by `label_store.py build` from config `structure`; report the opening cut point with the transcript lines before/after it.
